# Student Pass (Visa) Renewal for Returning International Students

## Leaving Malaysia
International students who defer, withdraw, are terminated or finish their studies must have their student pass shortened or cancelled before leaving Malaysia. Ask the Visa Office for the steps. Leaving without doing this can get your passport blacklisted by the Immigration Department.

## Overstayed or expired pass
Go to the Visa Office immediately, or call +60 3 8924 8257 or the hotline +60 3 8924 8088. Overstaying is a serious offence in Malaysia; clearing it can take up to 3 months and can disrupt your studies. You hold your own passport, so you are responsible for not overstaying.

## Who must renew
- Students moving from one year of a course to the next (for example Year 1 to Year 2) renew their student pass.
- Students moving from Foundation to undergraduate, or undergraduate to postgraduate, need a new eVAL instead (see Special Circumstances).
- Students with a pass for their whole course must still renew their i-Kad every year, or the pass may be revoked.

## Conditions for renewal (set by EMGS and Immigration)
- Attendance of at least 80%.
- A pass in all modules. If you failed or did poorly in any module, you need a supporting letter from the Dean and a study plan.
- Immigration can approve or refuse any renewal. Refusals can be appealed.

## When and how to renew
- Submit for renewal at least 4 months before your pass expires.
- Send the Visa Office a digital colour copy of every page of your current passport (and your old passport if you recently renewed it).
- Your passport must be valid for at least 18 more months and have more than 8 blank pages; renew it at your embassy first if not.
- Undergraduate/PGT transcripts are requested from Student Registry. Research students need a progress and attendance report signed by the Dean, Head of School or supervisor.
- Submitting less than 14 working days before expiry means paying an extra RM100 for a special pass.
- You will be told when to hand in your passport. Processing usually takes 14-21 working days. Do not plan travel while your passport is with Immigration.

## Cost
Renewal fee RM1,400, paid through NottinghamHub (MyNottingham) after the pass is renewed. Bring the printed receipt when you collect your passport.

## Who cannot simply renew
- Students not progressing to the next year (interrupting studies): see the Visa Office.
- Students changing to a different course from the one on their VAL must apply for a new VAL. Course changes are only allowed in the first year.
- Students with a 3-year pass cannot change course while in Malaysia; they must cancel the pass, go home and reapply.
- Students transferring to the UK or China campus must cancel their pass before leaving.
- To cancel a pass, bring your original passport and a flight ticket dated at least one month after submission.

## Lost passport
1. Report it to the UNM Security Office (+603 8924 8065).
2. Tell the Visa Office (visarenewal@nottingham.edu.my, +603 8924 8078 or +603 8924 8088).
3. Get a confirmation letter from Student Registry.
4. Make a police report within 24 hours at the nearest police station.
5. Take the police report and confirmation letter to your embassy or high commission for a new passport, and give the Visa Office a copy the next day.
6. Bring your new passport to the Visa Office to transfer your student pass. A special pass fee may apply.

## After renewing your passport
Bring both old and new passports to the Visa Office straight away to transfer the visa sticker. A small fee is paid to EMGS. Make sure your embassy has marked the old passport as void.
