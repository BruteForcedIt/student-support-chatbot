# Timetabling and Room Booking FAQs

- When are classes held? Mostly 9am to 6pm on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, and 9am to 1pm on Wednesday. Wednesday afternoons are normally kept free for university activities.
- Why are there timetable clashes? Usually because of the mix of core and optional modules, or because students did not choose their optional modules in time (choices are loaded into the timetabling system in May).
- Why does the timetable change every year? Student numbers, staff, module choices and available rooms change.
- How are rooms chosen? By class size, room type and equipment needed.
- Leave a room 10 minutes before the next class starts.
- Building, activity and room codes: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/Timetabling/Building-Activity-Room-Codes.pdf
- How to read the timetable grid: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/Timetabling/gridtimetablesexplained.pdf
- Two groups booked into the same room? Check the room timetable online, or contact Timetable Services (ext. 8057, RoomBooking@nottingham.edu.my).
- Extra AV equipment: contact the Audio Visual team or the IT Service Desk (itservicedesk@nottingham.edu.my).
