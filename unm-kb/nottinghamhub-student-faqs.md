# NottinghamHub Student FAQs

NottinghamHub (Campus Solutions) is UNM's student administration system. Students use it to register, enrol on modules, update personal details, pay fees, request support and contact tutors. It also links to timetables, Blue Castle (results) and Moodle. It works in any browser, 24 hours a day, but is best on a laptop or desktop.

## Logging in and account
- Log in with your university IT username and password (the same as email and Moodle).
- Forgotten password or cannot log in: raise a ticket with the IT Service Desk (ITServiceDesk@nottingham.edu.my).
- Change your password through the IT Accounts portal. This also changes it for email, Moodle and other systems.
- Page keeps loading (spinning wheel): clear your browser cache, then try again. If it still fails, contact the IT Service Desk.
- Accessibility problems: contact the IT Service Desk.
- Change your university photo: contact the Security Office (security@nottingham.edu.my).
- Update contact details, addresses or emergency contacts: open the "Profile" tile.
- Find your student ID number: "Profile" tile, top left corner.

## Getting help through NottinghamHub
- The "Help" tile is used to request disability support, financial support, a change in study plan, official documents and extenuating circumstances.
- To talk to someone in person, visit your Faculty Office or the Student Services Centre.
- Your personal tutor's details are in the "My Tutors" tile under "My Associated Staff".

## Finance in NottinghamHub
- Pay fees: "Finance" tile, then "Pay amount due", choose the charges, then "Next".
- Pay a course deposit: "Finance" tile, outstanding deposits are listed in Finance Information; enter the amount and click "Next".
- Advance payment: "Finance" tile, "Pay amount due", "Select Campus to Pay", choose "Online Deposit Malaysia", enter any amount. Advance payments are automatically used for the next charges raised (tuition, accommodation or other).
- Optional purchases: "Finance" tile, "Optional Purchases", choose the item and confirm.
- Official receipts: "Finance" tile, "Payment History", then "Print Receipt".
- Charges and due dates: shown on the "Finance" tile.
- Scholarships or sponsorship: check "Account history" to see how aid changes the amount due.
- All transactions since 1 January are visible. New transactions may take time to appear while staff verify them.
- Refunds: email Finance-Refund@nottingham.edu.my.
- Finance Office: +6 (03) 8924 8607, Finance-Office@nottingham.edu.my (also for reallocating a payment to a different charge or paying in person).

## Registration
- Returning students must register online every year. Follow the "Invite to Online Registration" email (check Junk/Clutter) and click the banner in NottinghamHub.
- Download your offer letter: "Documents" tile, choose the folder, click "Offer Letter", then download.
- Apply for another course: "Apply" tile, "Apply for a course".
- Incoming mobility students keep their student ID and username, get a new @nottingham.edu.my email, and must bring their existing student ID card to Malaysia.

## Studies and records
- Timetable: "Modules (courses)" tile, then "Timetable Malaysia".
- Module choices: you are emailed when choices open; contact your school and discuss with your personal tutor or programme director. Changes are possible in the first two weeks of classes ("Change of Mind" period) and at the start of semester two.
- Exam timetable: published at least a month before the exam period; you are emailed when it is out.
- Grades and results: Blue Castle (bluecastle-my-results.nottingham.ac.uk).
- Request official documents (confirmation letters, transcripts): "Help" tile, choose "Official Document", select the document and submit. Student Registry replies within three working days.
- Extenuating circumstances claim: "Help" tile, choose "Extenuating Circumstances", pick the sub-type, fill in the summary and details, and submit.
- Self-referring an academic misconduct case: see the Academic Misconduct page.

## Postgraduate research
- "Research" tile shows supervisors, topic, study duration and related menus.
- "My supervisors" tile, "Advising notes" shows supervision meeting notes.
- "Student milestones" tile lists yearly steps (such as reviews), due dates and status. Ask your supervisor about specific milestones.

## Uploading documents
- Upload through the "Documents" tile after choosing the right folder.
- A notification stays until staff have verified your upload.
- Wrong document uploaded: you can remove it within 30 minutes. After that, upload the correct one and contact the IT Service Desk.

## Graduation
- When registration opens you get an email; use the link on the "Graduation" tile. No link: email the Graduation office.
- To defer, choose "No, I wish to defer" on the registration form. Your certificate and Diploma Supplement are then issued at the next main ceremony.
- Smaller "inter-ceremonies" are held in March and October for students who need their award sooner.
