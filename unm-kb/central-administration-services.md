# Central Administration Services: Where to Go for Help

A guide for students who are not sure which office handles what.

- Accommodation: living in on-campus halls (see Accommodation FAQs).
- Attendance: attendance monitoring rules and procedures.
- Careers Advisory Service: careers advice, resources and employability support.
- Fees and Scholarships: tuition fees and scholarship information.
- Graduate School: support for postgraduate students, including research training and community events.
- Governance Office: data protection, meaning how the university collects, uses and protects your personal data and your rights as a data subject (https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Utilities/DataProtection.aspx).
- International Student Support: help for new international students.
- IT Services: IT account, Wi-Fi, university email, printing, Moodle and other learning tools.
- Library: books, eBooks, journals and databases (via NUsearch), plus reference and research support.
- Non-academic facilities: sports, recreation and other campus amenities.
- NottinghamHub: registration, module enrolment, personal details and fee payments.
- Security, Safety and Community: UNM ID card, security patrols, lost and found, safety permits, vehicle parking permits, incident investigations and emergency response. Emergency numbers: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/2025/Emergency-Contact-Number.pdf
- Student Registry: online induction, confirmation letters, provisional transcripts and related services.
- Students' Association: student leaders, clubs, societies and student-led activities (https://www.sanottingham.org/).
- Term dates: semester, exam and other key dates (https://www.nottingham.edu.my/AboutUs/Datesandcampusinformation/Calendar/Index.aspx).
- Visa and Immigration: everything about student visas.
- Wellbeing: counselling, mental health advice and disability advice.
