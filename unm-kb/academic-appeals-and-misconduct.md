# Academic Appeals and Academic Misconduct

## Academic appeals
Students can appeal a decision by an academic body about progression, assessment or awards.
- UNM students must use the UNM Appeals Form and follow the UNM Appeals Procedure. Make sure you use the forms for the Malaysia campus.
  - Appeals Form: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/UNMC-Academic-Appeal-Form-1219r.docx
  - Appeals Procedure: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/Academic-Appeals-Procedure-UNM.pdf
  - Appeals FAQs: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/Academic-Appeals-Frequently-Asked-Questions.pdf
  - Appeal Review Request Form: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/academic-appeal-review-request-form-0916.doc
- University Appeals Policy: https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/qualitymanual/concerns-complaints-and-appeals/pol-academic-appeals.aspx
- Questions about the appeals process: Academic Appeals team, Academic.Appeals@nottingham.edu.my
- Help with your specific case (whether you have a case, filling in the paperwork): Student Association Education Officer, saeducation@nottingham.edu.my

## Academic misconduct
- The Regulations on Academic Misconduct (in force since 1 August 2013) apply to students on all campuses: https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/qualitymanual/assessment-awards-and-deg-classification/pol-academic-misconduct.aspx
- Cases started on or before 31 July 2013 fall under the older Academic Offences Policy.
- To self-refer an academic misconduct case, use the guidance on this page or contact Student Registry.

## Quality Manual
The University's Quality Manual is the main source for study regulations, assessment, extenuating circumstances, academic offences and progression rules.
