# Learning Support Services

Learning Support offers one-to-one support for registered UNM students with learning differences, including:
- Dyslexia
- Dyscalculia
- Dyspraxia
- ADD / ADHD
- Asperger's Syndrome

You will need documents or medical evidence showing the assessment and diagnosis.
