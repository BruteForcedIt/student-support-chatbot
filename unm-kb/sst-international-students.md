# 6% Service Tax (SST) on Fees for Non-Malaysian Students

Since 1 July 2025, Malaysian law requires a 6% Service Tax on higher education services for non-Malaysian citizens. UNM collects it for the government.

## Who pays
- Non-Malaysian citizens: international students, foreign nationals and permanent residents (MyPR).
- Malaysian citizens (MyKad holders) do not pay it.
- Fees fully paid (cleared in the university's account) before 1 July 2025 are not taxed. The clearance date counts, not the date you started the payment.

## Taxed
Tuition; application, enrolment and registration fees; lab or material fees; international student fees; letters, transcripts and courier fees; reactivation fees; resits and repeated modules; short courses and executive education.

## Not taxed
On-campus accommodation, refundable security deposits, visa processing fees, and study trips not billed as tuition.

## How it is calculated
- 6% of the net tuition fee, after scholarships, waivers and discounts (including the Early Bird Discount).
- Applied to each instalment (for example RM300 on a RM5,000 instalment).
- Calculated in RM and paid together with your fees, not separately.
- Not refundable if you withdraw.
- The rate follows any future government changes.

## Special cases
- Formal exchange students billed by their home campus (UK or China): no SST.
- Fees fully paid by an external sponsor: no SST.
- 100% UNM-funded scholarship: no SST.
- No exemption exists for any other student group, including disabled (OKU) students.
- SST has no effect on your visa or immigration status.
