# Examinations and Exam FAQs

The Exams Office organises all written exams. Exams take place at the main campus in Semenyih, except some MBA exams. International students may be allowed to sit August/September reassessment (resit) exams in their home country at a university-approved centre; this does not apply to Foundation programmes.

## Adjusted exam arrangements
Special exam arrangements can be made for students with:
- specific learning difficulties (for example dyslexia, dyspraxia, ADHD)
- physical or mobility impairments
- hearing or visual impairments
- long-term medical or mental health conditions
Requests need evidence. Book an appointment with Wellbeing Services as early as possible.

## Exam FAQs
- Which exams am I entered for? Your exam dockets are based on the modules in your Module Entry Form (MEF). Check with your Faculty/School office. Fill in your MEF carefully: after the timetable is published, exam entries cannot be changed.
- When is the exam timetable released? About 4-6 weeks before exams: early December for Autumn exams and mid-March for Spring exams. It is published on the website and individual dockets are sent to your university email.
- Registered for the wrong exams? Tell the Exams Office immediately. If an exam is missing, still attend at the published time and place with your Student ID card and tell an invigilator; they will find you a seat.
- Two exams in one day or on consecutive days? This is normal university practice because of the number of exams and students. The office tries to avoid back-to-back exams and allows no more than two exams per day.
- Can the exam location change? Very rarely. If it does, affected students are told separately.
- Exam not on my timetable? It is probably an in-class exam set by your school; contact the module convenor.
- Results: from your Faculty/School office or Blue Castle (bluecastle-my-results.nottingham.ac.uk).
