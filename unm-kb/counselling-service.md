# University Counselling Service

The University Counselling Service (UCS) is free and confidential for all registered undergraduate and postgraduate students and for university staff. It is registered with the Malaysian Board of Counsellors (Reg No. 00090) and follows the Malaysian Counsellor's code of ethics. It cannot provide counselling that is part of an academic course, or counselling for staff members' families.

Counselling helps when something is making you unhappy and does not go away, or when it is hard to talk to people you know. It can help you make choices, make changes or feel less confused.

## Issues the service can help with
- Personal and emotional: adjusting to university, self-esteem and identity, anxiety, depression, phobias, suicide attempts.
- Academic: exam anxiety, study skills, language difficulties, doubts about your degree, low motivation, plans after university.
- Relationships: family, bereavement, children, friends, loneliness, separation or divorce, relationship breakdown.
- Other: alcohol and drugs, eating difficulties, rape and sexual assault, abuse, physical health, HIV, disability, bullying, pregnancy.

## Booking an appointment
- Walk in during opening hours, or book by email or phone.
- Before your first session you register with the service and fill in a confidential Client Information Form.
- Appointments are made within 24 hours.

## Confidentiality and records
- What you say is kept confidential: the service does not share spoken or written information without your permission.
- In rare cases, information may be shared without your consent if there is good reason to believe you or someone else is at risk.
- Counsellors keep only brief, factual notes, stored securely and locked within the Counselling Service.
- If you want to know more about how this works, ask your counsellor.
- Full confidentiality policy: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Wellbeing/Counselling/Confidentiality/Confidentiality.aspx
