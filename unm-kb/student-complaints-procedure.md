# Student Complaint Procedure

Complaints go through three internal levels. After those are finished, a student can ask the Office of the Independent Adjudicator (OIA) to review the case. Formal complaints are normally completed within 90 calendar days from the start of Level 2. Missing a deadline can stop you moving to the next level.

## Level 1: informal resolution (always start here)
- Raise the issue directly with the person or department involved, or with another staff member such as your personal tutor, module convenor or school manager.
- Do this within 1 month of the problem happening; the sooner the better.
- Explain clearly what went wrong and what outcome you want.
- You should get a response within 6 weeks (or be told a new timescale).

## Level 2: Head of School/Service formal review
- Fill in the Level 2 Complaint Form within 1 month of the Level 1 outcome: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/level-2-complaint-form-UNM.docx
- Explain how you tried to resolve it at Level 1: the date, the staff member you spoke to, and why it was not resolved.
- Attach evidence at the same time, clearly labelled (for example a timeline, relevant policies, emails, letters, medical evidence, witness statements, screenshots). Translate anything not in English.
- Complaints are rejected if the reason is unclear, evidence is missing, or key dates and details are left out.
- Email the form to Student.Complaints@nottingham.edu.my. Receipt is acknowledged within 5 working days; the outcome normally comes within 6 weeks.

## Level 3: Faculty Dean / Registrar review
- Submit the Level 3 Complaint Form within 10 working days of the Level 2 outcome: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/level-3-complaint-form-UNM.docx
- Allowed grounds: a procedural error at Level 2; new evidence that was not available before (with proof of why); or a well-evidenced argument that the Level 2 outcome was unreasonable. Simply disagreeing is not enough.
- Email it to Student.Complaints@nottingham.edu.my. The outcome normally comes within 4 weeks.

## After the internal process
- If the complaint is not upheld at the final stage, you receive a "Completion of Procedures" letter (within 28 days). You need it to go to the OIA.
- You can take the complaint to the OIA within 12 months of that letter: http://www.oiahe.org.uk/

## Other points
- You may bring a friend or a Students' Union representative to formal meetings. Lawyers may not attend.
- Paper forms are available if a disability stops you using the electronic forms; email Student.Complaints@nottingham.edu.my.
- Policy: https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/qualitymanual/concerns-complaints-and-appeals/pol-student-complaints.aspx
