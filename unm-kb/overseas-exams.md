# How to Apply for Overseas Examinations

Start arranging as soon as your school tells you your results; do not wait for an official letter. You must apply separately for every exam period you want to sit overseas. Overseas arrangements are not made for students who are simply on holiday or temporarily abroad during exam periods.

## Before applying
1. Check whether each of your modules can be taken overseas and whether it needs permission. Get approval from the school or department that runs each module, and note the names of the staff who approved it. Without the right permissions your application may be refused and you would sit the exam in Malaysia.
2. Contact an approved overseas institution yourself and ask if it can host your exams. If there is no approved institution reasonably near you, contact the Exams Office straight away. The university contacts the institution after the application deadline.
3. Apply by emailing exams@nottingham.edu.my before the published deadline. Late applications are not accepted.

## After applying
- The Exams Office arranges things with your school and the institution.
- Overseas exams must run at the same time as in Malaysia. If that is impossible, the Exams Office will give you an alternative time; if it cannot, you may need to return to Malaysia.
- One week before exams, check your exam docket (sent to your university email) shows your chosen institution.
- Before your first exam, confirm final arrangements with the institution and pay any local fees.
- Bring your University ID card to every exam.

## Cancelling
To sit in Malaysia instead, email the Exams Office at least 10 days before the exam period starts. Otherwise you still pay the full overseas exam fee of RM300 plus any local institution fees.

## Results
Released through Blue Castle; you are notified by university email.
