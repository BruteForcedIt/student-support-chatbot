# Attendance Policy (UNM)

UNM's attendance policy makes sure students attend required teaching and meetings, and meets Malaysian regulatory requirements.

## Key rules
- Taught students (Foundation, undergraduate and taught postgraduate) must attend at least 80% of scheduled classes.
- Postgraduate research students must attend supervision meetings: at least 10 per year full-time, or 6 per year part-time.
- Missing a class for any reason, including other university activities, does not excuse you from the work covered.
- Apply to your school in advance for planned absences, and tell your school/faculty straight away about unplanned absences, with evidence (for example a medical certificate or police report). Absence requests without suitable evidence, or submitted late, are rejected.
- International students who fall below the attendance requirement must be reported to the authorities and may have their student pass (visa) cancelled and studies ended.
- This policy is not for missed exams or coursework; use Extenuating Circumstances for those.

## Students' responsibilities
- Attend all scheduled classes, supervisions and required activities.
- Tell your school promptly if something stops you attending, and request authorised absence.
- Submit supporting documents to the Faculty Office within the school's timeframes.
- Attend any Attendance Intervention Meeting the school sets.
- Read the attendance rules in the Quality Manual and your School Handbook.

## How attendance is recorded
Attendance for taught classes is recorded in the Instatt app; research supervision meetings are recorded as milestones in Campus Solutions (NottinghamHub).
