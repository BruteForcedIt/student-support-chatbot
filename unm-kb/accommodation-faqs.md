# On-Campus Accommodation FAQs

Accommodation Office: accommodation@nottingham.edu.my, +603 8924 8604

## Applying
- Everyone applies through the Online Accommodation Application (OLAA) portal: https://applyaccommodation.nottingham.edu.my/
- New students can apply once they have a Nottingham student ID.
- Returning students should apply in the spring semester for the next academic year; the dates are announced by email.
- Places are not guaranteed, so apply early. Priority order: applicants aged 17 or under and applicants with disabilities first; then Foundation and first-year undergraduates; then second, third and fourth years; then postgraduates. Within each group it is first come, first served.
- You are told your status through OLAA. No reply after two weeks: email the Accommodation Office.
- If you do not get your preferred room type, accept what is offered and apply for a room transfer after check-in.

## Students with disabilities or special needs
Wheelchair-friendly rooms are available on ground floors. Say so in your OLAA application and attach medical reports; these go to Wellbeing and Learning Support for assessment.

## Check-in
- Tell the office your arrival date at least 3 working days ahead using the online check-in form.
- Check-in hours: Monday to Friday, 9am to 7pm (closed weekends and holidays). Outside these hours, wait for confirmation from the office first.
- Check in and collect keys at the Accommodation Office at Radius (Block J) or Tioman Hall (Block I).

## What rent includes
Wi-Fi, water and electricity, the first 100 kWh of air conditioning per month, and housekeeping and maintenance. Air conditioning above 100 kWh costs RM0.56 per kWh, billed every quarter.

## Rooms and what to bring
Rooms are fully furnished (bed and mattress, desk and chair, wardrobe, shelves, fan, curtains, basin, mirror, water heater, and air conditioning in most rooms). Each floor has a microwave, induction cooker and hot/cold water dispenser. Bring cups, plates, cutlery, a pail and bedding, or buy the bedding pack (RM180). A welcome pack of toiletries is given at check-in.

## Paying rent
Rent is billed quarterly. Pay through NottinghamHub (card, online banking, GrabPay or Touch 'n Go), by bank transfer (send the slip to finance-office@nottingham.edu.my), by card at the Finance Counters, by cheque, or by Flywire (international students). Cash is not accepted. Use the bank details printed on your official invoice.
- Checking in before the 16th: full month's rent. From the 16th: half a month (credited against the next bill).
- Checking out before the 16th: half a month refunded. From the 16th: full month charged.
- Sponsored students should still pay to secure the room; it is refunded once the sponsor confirms. Check with the Sponsorship Office.

## Living in halls
- Parking: apply for a Vehicle Parking Permit on the apps.nottingham portal (Security section). Spaces are limited and illegally parked cars are clamped.
- Visitors: only visitors of the same gender may enter your room, and they must leave by midnight. No visitors in rooms between midnight and 8am, and no overnight guests. Breaking these rules can lead to parents being informed and eviction.
- Maintenance problems: report through MaintainX (link on the FAQ page); you get email updates.
- Wi-Fi: connect to "eduroam" with your university username followed by @nottingham.edu.my and your university password (on phones choose PEAP, CA certificate "Do not validate", Phase 2 MSCHAPv2).
- Short stays for couples and small families: studio and guest house rooms are available; email the Accommodation Office with dates, number of guests and purpose.
- Halls are on campus: Student Village North (Block J, J1-J6) and Student Village South (Block I, I1-I5).
- Full rules: Accommodation Handbook (https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Study/Documents/Accommodation/Digital-Accommodation-Handbook-2023.pdf).

## Moving out early or cancelling
1. Submit the Check Out Form on the Student Support Hub (verbal notice is not accepted) and complete the self room inspection.
2. Return your key with its tag to the office or key drop box.
3. Remove all belongings and settle any outstanding fees.
4. Claim any overpayment with the Refund Form.
If you cancel after paying advance rent, you get a full refund if your application was withdrawn for academic reasons, you did not meet the entry requirements, or you were disqualified.
