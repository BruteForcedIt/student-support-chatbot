# Fees and Finance Overview and FAQ

## 2026/2027 tuition fee due dates
| Course | Semester | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation, Undergraduate and Postgraduate Taught (existing students) | Autumn | 14 October 2026 |
| Foundation, Undergraduate and Postgraduate Taught (existing students) | Spring | 10 February 2027 |
| Foundation | Spring | 14 April 2027 |
These dates do not apply to PGT-by-module, offshore, PGR progression or sponsored students.

- Late payment charge: 5% per year on the outstanding balance, minimum RM100.
- Early Bird Discount: 1.5% for paying early (see the Early Bird Discount page).
- Guide to checking your charges and due dates: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Study/Documents/Finance/2022/USER-GUIDE-CHECKING-CHARGES-AND-DUE-DATES.pdf

## FAQ
- Paying miscellaneous fees (resits, books, visas): through NottinghamHub. Guide: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Study/Documents/Finance/2022/USER-GUIDE-PAYING-OTHER-FEES-AND-PRINT-RECEIPT.pdf
- EPF withdrawal or PTPTN loan: see the Scholarships and Financial Aid page (https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Study/Fees-and-Scholarships/Scholarships/Financial-aid.aspx).
- Credit card Easy Payment Plans (IPP): offered by selected card providers under the Special Financial Aid option (https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Study/Documents/Finance/2026/Special-Financial-Aid-2026.pdf).
- Refund of overpaid fees (tuition, accommodation, visa or other): submit the e-Refund Form. Students and staff log in at https://apps.nottingham.edu.my/jw/web/login. See also the Refund Policy page.
- Official payment advice for tuition: email the Finance Office with your full name, student ID, course, and whether you need it for the full year or by semester. International students can ask for it in USD, GBP or EUR.
- International bank transfer not showing after two weeks: check the beneficiary name was exactly "The University of Nottingham in Malaysia Sdn Bhd". If it still does not show, email the Finance Office your MT103 transfer slip (date, amount, currency, sender and recipient). From 2026, international transfers go to the university's CIMB account only.
- Use an overpayment on your tuition account for accommodation or other fees: email the Finance Office to ask for an adjustment.
- Payment keeps failing on NottinghamHub: check your bank's daily limits and clear your browser cache, then try again later. If it shows as failed but money left your account, do not pay again; email the Finance Office.
- PGT students paying by module: the Finance Office emails your fee and due dates for your registered modules.
- Transferring to Nottingham UK: contact the UK Finance Office at tuitionfees@nottingham.ac.uk.
