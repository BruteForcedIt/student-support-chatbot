# Careers Advisory Service (Jobs, Internships and Career Advice)

The Careers Advisory Service (CAS) helps students plan their careers and look for work.

## Contact
- Enquiries and appointments: careers@nottingham.edu.my
- Location: Rooms HB14 and HB15, Block H, Level 1
- Opening hours: Monday to Friday, 9.00am to 5.30pm
- Main university line: +603 8924 8000

## Looking for a job or internship
- Log in to MyCareer for Students (https://mycareer.nottingham.edu.my/students) to explore job and internship opportunities, book a CV review or a career advice appointment, sign up for careers events and workshops, and read careers leaflets.
- Career advising: help with job-search strategies and an action plan.
- CAS also helps with searching for vacancies, internships, volunteering and work experience.
- Looking for part-time work: email CAS at careers@nottingham.edu.my or book a career advice appointment in MyCareer, and ask what part-time opportunities are available.

## Other support
- Career mentoring: industry exposure through alumni and employer mentors.
- Careers Resource Room: printed careers resources and publications.
- Careers events and workshops throughout the year, including a careers and internship fair.
- Virtual work experience programmes: free online taste of industry experience.
- Online career tools and e-leaflets for career planning and job searching.
- Over 60 clubs and societies on campus help students build skills employers value.

## International students
- One-to-one guidance on the skills and experience recruiters look for, and help finding vacancies, internships, volunteering and work experience in Malaysia or at home.
- Working in Malaysia has immigration rules for international students, and they apply to any job, including part-time work. Before taking a job, contact the International Office, which advises on work applications and employment passes.
