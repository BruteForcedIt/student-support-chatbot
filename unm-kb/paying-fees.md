# How to Pay Tuition Fees

Fees are charged in Ringgit Malaysia (RM). Cash is not accepted.
International student page: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/finance-fees/paying-your-fees/international-students.aspx

## Payment schedule
| Level | Option 1 | Option 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 100% of the year's fee at the start of Semester 1 | 50% at the start of Semester 1 and 50% at the start of Semester 2 |
| Undergraduate | 50% at the start of Semester 1 and 50% at the start of Semester 2 | |
| Postgraduate (Masters, general) | 50% each semester | |
| Selected PGT (e.g. Education, Psychology, EMBA) | Minimum on registration (RM3,000 Malaysian / RM3,180 international), balance once modules are chosen | |
| Selected Masters (e.g. Humanities, Chemical and Environmental Engineering) | 25% of the course fee each semester, finished within 2 years | |
| Research degrees (MRes, MPhil, PhD) | 50% each semester | |

## Extra fees for postgraduate research students
- Thesis-pending (writing up) registration: RM850
- Extension to thesis submission date: RM850 per application
- MPhil/MRes re-examination: RM850
- PhD re-examination: RM1,200

## Ways to pay
- NottinghamHub (preferred): pay online by Visa or Mastercard.
- Bank or telegraphic transfer: use the official bank details on the payment page (https://apps.nottingham.edu.my/jw/web/app/fo_tuition_fee/resources/MODE_OF_PAYMENT_2025.pdf). Always copy bank details from the official university page, never from a message or email you were sent. International transfers can take 2 weeks to process.
- Finance Counter (Trent Building, Block A, or Student Services Centre, Block H): debit or credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay) or QR payment (DuitNow, Alipay).
- Crossed cheque or bank draft payable to "The University of Nottingham in Malaysia Sdn Bhd".
- International students can also pay in their own currency through Flywire.
- After a bank transfer, cheque or draft, email proof of payment to Finance-Office@nottingham.edu.my with your ID number, what the payment is for, and the sender's name.
- International students paying in another currency cover any exchange shortfall and bank charges.

## Instalment plans
Students in financial difficulty can apply to pay tuition by instalments, depending on the programme. Available from Semester 2; apply on or before the payment deadline through the Student Support Hub (https://supporthub.nottingham.edu.my/). Late requests are not considered.

## Late payment
A 5% per year late charge applies to overdue balances, minimum RM100.

## Banking restrictions
The university's banks cannot accept funds from countries on their sanctions list (listed on the international payment page).
