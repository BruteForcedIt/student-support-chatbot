# Mental Health Advisory Service

A free and confidential service for students and staff with mental health and wellbeing concerns. Problems such as anxiety and depression can affect anyone, and getting support makes a real difference.

## What the service offers
- Assessments to understand the problem and the support needed
- One-to-one support
- Referral letters to internal and external professionals
- Raising awareness of mental health

## Common signs
- Anxiety: feeling on edge, racing heart, nervous habits, trembling, tiredness.
- Depression: avoiding friends, loss of appetite, low energy, intense sadness, irritability.

## Confidentiality
The service is confidential and will ask your permission before arranging appointments with a psychiatrist or other professionals. If staff believe you are at serious risk to yourself or others, this overrides confidentiality and the relevant people will be contacted.
