# Hardship Fund

Since 2011 UNM has offered a Hardship Fund for students facing unexpected financial difficulty because their circumstances changed significantly after they were admitted. Each application is judged individually by a committee.

Before applying, talk to the Finance Office about payment plans and to the Careers Advisory Service about work opportunities.

## Who can apply
- Registered, active students
- With outstanding tuition fees
- Facing unexpected financial difficulty

## How to apply
Send the completed form with all supporting documents to the Sponsorship Unit before a deadline. Deadlines have been held every two months, on the 15th of January, March, May, July, September and November (confirm the current year's dates with the Sponsorship Unit).
