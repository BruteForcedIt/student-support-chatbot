# Religious Observance During Exams

The university respects students' religious beliefs. If religious observance means you cannot sit exams on certain days during the exam periods:

1. Fill in the Religious Observance Form: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/CurrentStudents/documents/Examinations/religious-observance-form.pdf
2. Have it signed by your personal tutor (or equivalent).
3. Submit it to your School Examinations Officer by the published deadline for that exam period.

You only need to submit the form once, ideally when you start your studies; it stays on your record. Always check your module records when asked, because the exam timetable is built from them. (The deadlines on the page are for past years, so check current deadlines with your School Examinations Officer.)

If your request cannot be met, contact your School Examinations Officer. Possible alternatives include a different exam paper on another day, sitting the exam earlier or later with a chaperone approved by the school, or another form of assessment. In exceptional cases the school may offer a deferral to the August/September resit period under the Extenuating Circumstances Policy.
