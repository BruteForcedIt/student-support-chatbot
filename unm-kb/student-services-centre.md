# Student Services Centre

The Student Services Centre is the main counter for student services.
Location: HB13, Block H, Student Association building. Counter hours: Monday to Friday, 9.30am to 5.00pm.
Email: Student_ServicesCentre@nottingham.edu.my

| Department | What it helps with |
|---|---|
| Finance Office | All payments: tuition, accommodation, fines, rental, reprinting official documents |
| Accommodation | On-campus accommodation queries |
| Visa | Student visa matters: new applications, progression and renewal |
| Student Registry | Letters and academic transcripts; reprints of graduation certificates and official transcripts (fees may apply); updating student details; verifying and certifying documents (bring the original) |
| Sponsorship | PTPTN applications and queries, EPF withdrawal, sponsored students (e.g. MARA and external sponsors), insurance (local students only), internal scholarships (Dean's Excellence Award, High Achievers, Sibling Scholarship) |
