# Disability Advisory Services

The Disability Advisory Services supports equal opportunity for disabled students by arranging reasonable adjustments, so students can complete their courses as independently as possible.

## Who counts as disabled
- Seen disabilities: physical, visual or hearing impairment.
- Unseen disabilities: mental health conditions or long-term medical conditions (lasting 12 months or more) that affect day-to-day activities.

## Eligibility
Registered students with a long-term (12 months or more) disability, supported by recent medical evidence (see the Medical Evidence Policy: https://www.nottingham.edu.my/Wellbeing/Disability/MedicalEvidencePolicy.aspx).

## What the service can help with
- Admissions and registration for students with disabilities
- Asking academic staff for reasonable adjustments in teaching, learning and assessment
- Recommending alternative assessment and timetabling arrangements (students needing alternative exam arrangements, especially for physical or mobility needs, should go through this service)
- Arranging enhanced library services such as extended loans
- Working with IT Services on computer lab accessibility
- Working with the Accommodation Office on on-campus housing
