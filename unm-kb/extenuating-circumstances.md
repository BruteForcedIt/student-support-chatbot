# Extenuating Circumstances (EC) Claims

If you miss an assessment, or think a serious problem affected your performance in one, submit an Extenuating Circumstances (EC) form through NottinghamHub.

## How to submit
1. Log in to NottinghamHub with your university credentials.
2. Open the "Academic Support" tile.
3. In the left-hand menu, choose "Extenuating Circumstances".
4. Follow the on-screen steps to complete and submit the form.

## Deadlines
- Coursework: submit the EC claim before the original coursework deadline.
- Exams: ideally submit before the exam starts. It must be submitted within five working days after the exam, and only if the mark has not yet been released to you.
- Claims must be made within these timeframes.

## Evidence you must attach
Examples of acceptable evidence:
- a medical certificate
- a letter from a suitable medical professional
- a letter from University Wellbeing Services
- a written statement about a crime, backed by written evidence from the police
