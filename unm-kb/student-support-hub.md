# Student Support Hub

The UNM Student Support Hub is a single portal for student services. Students can submit requests at any time, track them until they are resolved, and find FAQs, guides and help articles. It also has forms for campus living needs such as transport, rooms, keys and inspections.

## How to get started
1. Go to supporthub.nottingham.edu.my, or install the Freshservice app (Android or iOS) and enter supporthub.nottingham.edu.my as the address.
2. Sign in with your UNM student email (single sign-on).
3. Browse Help Articles, or choose "Request a Service" or "Report an Issue".
4. Track and manage all your requests in one place.
