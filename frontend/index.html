How the Support Buddy works

What it can help with

Questions about university services: exams and extenuating circumstances, fees and payments, visas, accommodation, official documents, wellbeing and how to make a complaint.

Where answers come from

Answers are written by an AI using only documents uploaded by university staff. Each answer lists its sources. If the documents do not cover your question, the Buddy says so instead of guessing.

Getting a better answer

  • Ask one thing at a time, in your own words.
  • Choose a category on the left to focus the search.
  • Use the thumbs up or down under an answer to tell us if it helped.

Your privacy

Conversations are not saved on our servers (this browser tab keeps your chat until you close it). Do not type passwords, card numbers or other private details. The Buddy cannot see your student record.
