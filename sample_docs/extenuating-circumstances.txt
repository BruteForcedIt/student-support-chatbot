Students can submit an extenuating circumstances claim if illness, bereavement or another serious event affects an assessment.
The claim form must be submitted within 7 days of the assessment deadline.
You must attach evidence, for example a medical certificate from a registered doctor.
Claims without evidence will not be considered. The Student Registry will email the outcome within 10 working days.
